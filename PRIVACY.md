# Privacy Policy

Reddit Jr. is a personal, open source Reddit client. It has no server of its own, no account system, no ads and no analytics or tracking libraries.

## What stays on your device
Everything the app stores is kept on your phone: your Reddit login tokens, settings, subscriptions, history, recently visited lists, drafts and crash reports. Nothing is sent to the developer. `Settings > Backup & Restore` writes a file only where you tell it to.

## Who the app talks to
The app connects directly from your phone to these services, and only to do what you ask:
- **Reddit** (reddit.com and oauth.reddit.com), with the Client ID you enter, to log in, load and send posts, comments, votes, messages and so on. Reddit's own [privacy policy](https://www.reddit.com/policies/privacy-policy) covers that data.
- **Media hosts** such as i.redd.it, v.redd.it, Imgur, Redgifs, Streamable and Giphy, when a post links to their content. They see your IP address, as with any browser.
- **Arctic Shift**, only when you use Recover Post on a removed post.
- **GitHub** (api.github.com and github.com), to check for and download updates to this app. Turn this off in `Settings > About`.
- **Google Lens and Yandex**, only when you choose Reverse image search. The image's web address is passed to them in your browser.

## Permissions
The app asks for network access, notifications, storage and wallpaper access (to save or set images), the camera (to attach photos to posts) and permission to install updates. Each is used only for the matching feature.
